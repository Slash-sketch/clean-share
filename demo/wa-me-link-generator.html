wa.me link generator

A wa.me link opens WhatsApp to a phone number with a message already filled in. For example, https://wa.me/923001234567?text=Hi opens a chat with that number and the text "Hi".

How to use it

Open the demo, enter the phone number (with country code, for example +92 300 1234567), type your message, and paste the link you want to share. The demo cleans the link first — tracking parameters are removed — then builds the WhatsApp link for you.

Open the Clean-share demo

You can also share the same cleaned link by Telegram, SMS or email from the demo.

No upload. Everything runs in your browser, on your device. You can check this in your browser's DevTools Network tab — Clean-share makes no network requests. Clean-share does not check whether a phone number is registered on WhatsApp.
