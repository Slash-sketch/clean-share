Remove utm from a link

Marketing links carry utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term and utm_content parameters. They do not change the page — they only track where you came from. Clean-share removes every parameter starting with utm_, plus other known trackers such as fbclid, gclid and igshid.

Example

Before:
https://example.com/page?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&id=7

After:
https://example.com/page?id=7

The page id (id) is kept — only the trackers are removed. Clean-share reports every removal, so you can see exactly what changed.

What it never strips

Signed links are returned completely untouched. If a link carries sig, token, expires or an X-Amz-* parameter, nothing is removed — stripping one of those would break the link.
