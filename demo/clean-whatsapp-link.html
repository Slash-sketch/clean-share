Clean a WhatsApp link

Links shared on WhatsApp often carry tracking parameters like utm_source, fbclid or si. They make the link long and tell the site where you came from. Clean-share strips those parameters in your browser — the link itself, the video, the product, stays the same.

Example

Before:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&si=abc123

After:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ

The video id (v) is kept — only the tracker (si) is removed.

Try it

Open the Clean-share demo

Paste your link, copy the clean link, and share it with the WhatsApp button. You can also add a phone number and a message, and the demo builds a wa.me link for you.

No upload. Everything runs in your browser, on your device. You can check this in your browser's DevTools Network tab — Clean-share makes no network requests.
